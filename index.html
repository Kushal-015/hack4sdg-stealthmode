How ECO Bengaluru works

A clear chain of custody helps households, collectors and verification teams work together.

STEP 01

Register household

Receive a unique Smart Trash Card with a secure QR identity.

STEP 02

Segregate waste

Keep wet and dry waste separate in individual bags.

STEP 03

Scan & tag

Collector records category and weight, then prints a unique bag tag.

STEP 04

Verify the bag

Verification staff inspect and approve or reject the collection record.

STEP 05

Earn points

Eligible points are credited once, after successful verification.
