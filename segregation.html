KNOW YOUR WASTE

Choose the Right Category

Keep different types of waste separate at home.

🟢
WET WASTE

Organic Waste

Biodegradable waste from your kitchen and food preparation.

  • 🍌 Fruit and vegetable peels
  • 🍚 Leftover food
  • ☕ Tea leaves and coffee grounds
  • 🥚 Eggshells
  • 🍂 Garden leaves
Tip:

Keep food waste separate from plastic and paper.

🔵
DRY WASTE

Recyclable Materials

Clean, dry materials that can often be reused or sent for recycling.

  • 📰 Newspapers and paper
  • 🥤 Clean plastic bottles
  • 📦 Cardboard boxes
  • 🥫 Metal cans
  • 🍾 Glass bottles
Tip:

Empty and keep recyclable packaging dry.

🟠
SPECIAL WASTE

Handle Separately

Items that need special collection or careful disposal.

  • 🔋 Batteries
  • 💡 Used electronic items
  • 💊 Expired medicines
  • 🧴 Paint or chemical containers
  • 🩹 Sanitary and medical waste
Tip:

Follow local collection rules. Never mix hazardous items with ordinary waste.

🌱 ECO Bengaluru's Golden Rule

Segregate waste at the source, keep different categories separate, and follow your local municipal collection guidelines.
